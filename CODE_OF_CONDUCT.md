# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and safe community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and precise.
- Assume good faith while accepting correction.
- Focus criticism on ideas, code, documentation, and evidence—not people.
- Protect private data, credentials, and security reports.
- Respect maintainers' decisions and community boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption, unauthorized disclosure of private information, and attempts to bypass security or approval controls are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's GitHub Security page or another private channel provided by a maintainer. Do not include sensitive vulnerability details in a public issue. Maintainers may edit, close, or remove contributions and restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
